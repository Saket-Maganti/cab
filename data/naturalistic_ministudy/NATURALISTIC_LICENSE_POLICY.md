# Naturalistic Mini-Study License Policy

Status: policy only.

Allowed sources:

- self-authored local artifacts,
- permissively licensed public examples,
- generated synthetic documents that do not copy private data,
- benchmark-compatible open datasets with license recorded.

Disallowed sources:

- private email, documents, calendars, or code,
- scraped webpages without explicit permission,
- content with unclear redistribution rights,
- secrets, API keys, or credentials.

Every task must include a provenance note before execution.
