# C10 Isolation Review Packet

This packet prepares human review of intervention isolation. It contains templates only.

Labels: `engineering_only`, `manual_review_pending`, `no_provider_evidence`.

## Files

- `c10_isolation_annotation_template.csv`: independent reviewer sheet.
- `c10_adjudication_template.csv`: adjudication sheet after two completed reviews.
- `C10_REVIEWER_INSTRUCTIONS.md`: reviewer instructions and illustrative examples.

## Evidence Boundary

Blank templates do not support C10, C3, model-performance claims, paper assets, or NeurIPS readiness. Agreement must not be computed until at least two independent completed reviewer sheets exist.
