# Compact-20 Replacement Candidate Policy

Status: no-execution policy.

## Replacement Triggers

Replace a Compact-20 candidate only after review finds one of:

- high task clarity risk,
- high gold ambiguity risk,
- high scorer fragility risk,
- high intervention confounding risk,
- unavailable bundled evidence,
- privacy or licensing concern,
- family overrepresentation that weakens the sample.

## Replacement Priority

- `high`: exclude before any provider execution.
- `medium`: retain only if human review clears the issue.
- `low`: acceptable for pilot, not for headline claims.
- `none`: no replacement currently recommended.

## Replacement Constraints

- Preserve family balance unless the family itself is invalid.
- Prefer replacing within the same domain/difficulty/family stratum.
- Never use a replacement to improve expected results.
- Never infer replacement quality from AI-proxy labels.

## Current State

All replacement decisions remain pending real review. No candidate is marked human-reviewed by this policy.
