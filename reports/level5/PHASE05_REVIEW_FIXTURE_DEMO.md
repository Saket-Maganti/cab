# Phase 05 review fixture demo

The review service validates local-only binding, persists immutable judgments,
separates fixture/genuine counts and emits append-only audit entries. Assignment
tests require two independent qualified conflict-free reviewers. Agreement,
amendment, consent, proxy/AI rejection and adjudication paths are covered.

Fixture judgments are explicitly rejected by C10. Genuine count remains zero.

Acceptance: `CAB_HUMAN_REVIEW_OS_READY`; state
`HUMAN_VALIDATION_REQUIRED`.
