# Task Family Balance Plan

Status: no-execution sampling plan.

## Balance Rules

- Each primary intervention family should have a minimum quota.
- No single family should exceed 15% of Main-500 unless preregistered.
- Each domain should include both low-risk and high-risk intervention families where feasible.
- High scorer-risk families require extra human review before execution.

## Recommended Quotas

| scale | target families | minimum pairs per primary family | notes |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Compact-20 | 4-6 | 3 | review and pipeline only |
| 100-task | 10-12 | 8 | directional, wide CI |
| Main-500 | 20-25 | 20 | headline only after gates |

## Avoiding Easy-Family Overfit

Hold out at least 25% of families from early prompt/debug iteration. Keep an audit trail for any task replacement. Do not replace tasks based on provider performance.
