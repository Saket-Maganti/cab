\section{Human Validation}

The repository includes an export workflow (\texttt{export-human-validation}) that samples clean and intervention trajectories across domains, difficulties, intervention families, agents, and outcome types. Annotation forms, adjudication rules, and sampling guidelines are documented in \texttt{docs/HUMAN\_VALIDATION\_GUIDELINES.md}, \texttt{docs/HUMAN\_VALIDATION\_PROTOCOL.md}, and \texttt{docs/HUMAN\_VALIDATION\_FORM\_SCHEMA.md}. Annotators or expert auditors should judge whether the task is understandable, whether the intervention preserves the high-level user goal, whether the changed factor is isolated, whether the expected robust behavior is reasonable, whether final-answer labels are correct, and whether trajectory diagnostics reflect real failures.
