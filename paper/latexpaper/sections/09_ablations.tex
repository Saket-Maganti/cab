\section{Ablations}

compares \texttt{RAAC\_FULL} with \texttt{VERIFY\_ONLY},
\texttt{RETRY\_ONLY}, \texttt{ABSTAIN\_ONLY},
\texttt{NO\_CROSS\_CHECK}, \texttt{NO\_ALTERNATE\_ROUTE}, and
\texttt{NO\_FINAL\_VERIFY} under locked budgets. Existing prompt, planner,
memory-verification, scoring, and backend ablations remain secondary.
