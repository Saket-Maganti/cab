# Results Section Placeholder Policy

Status: active no-execution policy.

## Allowed Placeholder Text

- `RESULT_REQUIRED`
- `PROVIDER_RUN_REQUIRED`
- `HUMAN_REVIEW_REQUIRED`
- `C10_VALIDATION_REQUIRED`
- `PAPER_ASSET_NOT_ELIGIBLE`

## Forbidden

- invented sample sizes,
- invented ACRS/rank-instability values,
- provider comparisons from mock/stub outputs,
- human agreement statistics before annotations,
- “we find” or “we show” empirical claims before evidence.

## Required Table Notes

Every placeholder table must say that no provider-backed results have been executed and that values are intentionally absent.
