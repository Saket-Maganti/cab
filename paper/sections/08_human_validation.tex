\section{Human Validation}

Human validation is planned but not yet complete. The validation protocol should sample clean and intervention instances across domains, difficulties, intervention families, agents, and outcome types. Annotators or expert auditors should judge whether the task is understandable, whether the intervention preserves the high-level user goal, whether the changed factor is isolated, whether final-answer labels are correct, and whether trajectory diagnostics reflect real failures.

The paper should report agreement, adjudication procedure, annotator qualifications, compensation, and any systematic disagreements.
