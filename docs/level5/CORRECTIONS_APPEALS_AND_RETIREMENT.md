# Corrections, appeals and retirement

Corrections create a new immutable version that supersedes the old record.
Public notices identify affected task/run/result/claim hashes, impact and
resolution. Leaderboard or paper assets are regenerated from the corrected
evidence graph.

Contributors, reviewers and submitters may appeal acceptance, exclusion,
disqualification or contamination decisions. A non-conflicted reviewer records
the evidence and outcome. Appeals do not erase the original audit event.

Deprecated tasks remain comparable for their original version. Retirement stops
new evaluation; contamination additionally invalidates downstream eligibility.
Protected payloads follow retention policy rather than being copied into a
public retirement archive.
