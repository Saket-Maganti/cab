# Human review governance

Reviewers must give informed consent, disclose relevant expertise/conflicts and
attest that judgments are their own unaided human work. Authors may review only
under the declared authors-as-reviewers policy and never adjudicate their own
conflict.

Two independent qualified reviewers cover every item. A separate adjudicator
resolves disagreements. Replacement and amendment require logged reasons; old
rows remain in the audit trail.

Public exports use pseudonymous IDs and exclude the identity map. Private
identity/consent records follow the approved retention period and access log.
Compensation methods and aggregate rates are reported without identifying
individuals. Reviewers may appeal exclusion or amendment decisions to a
non-conflicted maintainer.
