# Anti-gaming policy

CAB audits protected-prompt echoes, encoded dumps, filesystem enumeration,
unusually large/high-entropy output, repeated probing, task-specific
hard-coding, abstention abuse, score-oracle queries and result-file changes.

Automatic disqualification requires a versioned rule and retained receipt.
Heuristic review flags require human security review. False positives may be
appealed with a reproducible submission hash. Probe limits, budgets and task
support are fixed before evaluation; they cannot be tuned to a submitter's
score.

Performance jumps, collusion and memorization are investigated through
contamination history and comparable-support analysis. Public leaderboards
include uncertainty and reproducibility status rather than an unqualified point
rank.
