# Novelty Boundary Memo

Labels: `engineering_only`, `manual_review_pending`, `no_provider_evidence`.

## Not The Novelty

- It is not novel to say agents fail.
- It is not novel to score tool-use tasks.
- It is not novel to run robustness benchmarks.
- It is not currently proven that CAB is causally valid.

## Possible Novelty Later

The defensible novelty may be the combination of:

- paired clean/intervention instances,
- named intervention taxonomy,
- explicit validity governance for C10,
- ranking-instability measurement comparing clean success to robustness-aware summaries,
- no-overclaim evidence gates.

## ACRS Boundary

ACRS is simple. It should be described as a compact robustness summary, not a causal estimator or a fundamentally new evaluation theory.

## Synthetic-Task Boundary

The template nature of the data is a limitation. The paper should say this plainly and treat naturalistic transfer as future work.

## Evidence Boundary

Real empirical novelty requires future provider-backed Compact-20/50 runs, scorer sanity checks, gold-policy review, and C10 human validation.
