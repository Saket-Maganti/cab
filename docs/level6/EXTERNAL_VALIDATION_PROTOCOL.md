# Independent Replication and External Audit Protocol

Replication tiers are `ASSISTED_REPRODUCTION`, `INDEPENDENT_REPRODUCTION`,
`BLIND_REPRODUCTION`, and `ALTERNATE_IMPLEMENTATION_REPRODUCTION`. Independence
requires separate personnel, no author-operated execution, no hidden-task
access, public artifacts unless protected access is governed, a signed receipt,
and complete environment and deviation records.

Audit packets are separately scoped for statistics, intervention validity,
scoring, governance, supply chain, and protected evaluation. Each packet binds
the request, immutable inputs, access level, auditor conflict statement,
findings, limitations, and signature. Authors cannot issue their own external
audit receipt.

Discrepancies are classified critical, major, moderate, or minor and assigned a
root cause: specification, data, implementation, environment, analysis,
governance, security, or unresolved. The record binds the finding, author
response, corrective action, verification, signed resolution, and unresolved
state. Unresolved critical or major findings block promotion.

No fixture packet increments an external-validation counter.
