# CAB Benchmark Constitution

Status: foundation constitution. CAB does not yet claim an active stewardship
board or a genuine board approval.

## Mission and scope

Causal Agent Bench measures bounded causal robustness of tool-using agents with
paired, controlled tasks. CAB may govern task authoring, interventions, scoring,
review, protected evaluation, evidence, releases, and corrections. It may not
convert fixtures into scientific evidence or claim capabilities outside frozen
construct and estimand definitions.

## Decision rights and responsibilities

Maintainers operate releases, safeguard protected material, enforce evidence
boundaries, disclose conflicts, and execute valid decisions. A future advisory
board may approve constitutional amendments, protected-pool policy, material
scorer changes, benchmark-version promotion, and emergency corrections. Until
that board is constituted by signed records, maintainers may prepare proposals
but must not record board approval.

Task stewards propose and document tasks. Review coordinators manage physically
separated review packets without judging their own proposals. Release stewards
verify provenance and signatures. Security stewards control protected access and
revocation. Statistical stewards own preregistered estimands, multiplicity, and
uncertainty policy.

## Conflicts, quorum, and voting

Every voter discloses financial, authorship, employment, evaluation, and close
collaboration conflicts. A conflicted member recuses from the affected vote and
does not count toward eligible quorum. Ordinary decisions require a majority of
eligible votes with at least half of eligible members participating. Scorer,
constitution, protected-pool, and benchmark-version decisions require two-thirds
of eligible non-conflicted votes. Ties fail closed. All votes and recusals are
content-bound in a signed decision record.

## Amendments

An amendment proposal must include the exact diff, rationale, affected claims,
migration plan, compatibility impact, public-comment window, conflict record,
and rollback plan. Acceptance creates a new constitution version. Amendments do
not retroactively change frozen results.

## Tasks and scorers

A task enters only after provenance, licence, semantic-fact, intervention,
solvability, leakage, and human-review gates. Removal requires a reason code and
preserves historical version references. Scorer changes require golden vectors,
adversarial tests, migration impact, independent review, and a benchmark version
change whenever results may differ.

## Versioning and emergency corrections

CAB uses immutable task-pack, scorer-policy, and release identifiers. Material
changes produce a new benchmark version. An urgent security or scientific error
may trigger a signed emergency correction, temporary suspension, public notice,
impact inventory, and later ratification. Silent replacement is forbidden.

## Appeals and disputes

Any submitter or contributor may file an evidence-bound appeal. A non-conflicted
panel acknowledges it, records scope, preserves the challenged receipt, invites
a response, and issues a signed outcome. Disputes use the same record and may be
escalated once. Corrections and revocations remain linked to the original result.

## Credit, forks, and transparency

Contribution records distinguish task authorship, review, engineering,
statistics, governance, security, and replication. Public forks are permitted by
the repository licence but must not imply CAB endorsement or reuse CAB protected
identifiers. Public logs include versions, decisions, corrections, conflicts,
retirements, and aggregate protected-pool changes without revealing protected
task bodies, reviewer identities, secrets, or private keys.

## Protected data

Protected material uses least privilege, encrypted transfer and storage,
time-bounded access, immutable audit receipts, rotation, revocation, incident
response, and deletion schedules. Stage 2 remains inaccessible until Stage-1
commitment. No protected task body or signing private key enters public Git.

## Succession, dissolution, and archival

Maintainers document at least two succession contacts once governance is active.
If stewardship becomes non-viable, CAB freezes submissions, preserves public
artifacts and signed histories, revokes live credentials, documents protected
data disposition, and publishes an archival notice. Dissolution cannot erase
valid historical corrections, licences, or contributor credit.
